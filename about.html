About My Cafe

From a simple Lab 11 digital menu to a complete community coffee house vision built on warmth, simplicity, and quality.

Our Brand Story

My Cafe began as a clean, welcoming web concept created by Anas Shahid (Roll No. 26i-3113, Section E) in Lab 11—celebrating honest coffee and tea favorites like Espresso, Cappuccino, Latte, Milk Tea, Green Tea, and Black Tea.

In Lab 12, that foundation grew into a comprehensive 10-page digital home. Rather than discarding our original beige (#F5F5DC), rich brown (#A52A2A), and forest green (#006400) identity, we expanded it into an accessible, thoughtfully structured experience that reflects how a true neighborhood cafe feels: unpretentious, inviting, and dependable.

Preserving our original Lab 11 Cappuccino centerpiece as the visual symbol of our craft.

Purpose

Our Mission

To serve consistently fresh, fairly priced coffee and tea in a calm, distraction-free environment where students, professionals, and neighbors feel genuinely at home.

Future

Our Vision

To bridge traditional tea warmth with modern specialty coffee education—making ethical sourcing, careful brewing, and community hospitality accessible to everyone.

Standards

Quality & Freshness

We prioritize freshly roasted beans, whole-leaf teas, calibrated water temperatures, and clean preparation standards across every single cup served.

Core Values

Value How We Practice It Daily
Honest Simplicity Clear menus, transparent pricing, and straightforward ingredients without unnecessary complexity.
Warm Hospitality Greeting every guest with patience and care, whether they stay for five minutes or three hours.
Craft & Consistency Measuring grind size, extraction time, and steeping temperature so your favorite drink tastes right every visit.
Community Focus Supporting student study sessions, small book clubs, and educational coffee tasting workshops.
